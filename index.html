Reading this map

  • 33% is the target. Green cells mean an evaluation started that month passed at least a third of the time. Amber and brown months are the ones to avoid buying into.
  • Attempts overlap. A new attempt starts every trading day and each runs 30 days, so a strong or weak stretch is counted many times. Treat a single cell as a hint, not a measurement.
  • The rules decide the outcome, not the brand. Plans with no consistency rule and a daily limit above about $1,000 sit near 31%. A 40–50% consistency rule roughly halves that, and a $500 daily limit ends it.
  • Grey chips flag rules taken from third-party sources. Confirm those on the firm's own dashboard before paying for an account.
  • This is backtest history, not a forecast. The whole window is the strategy's own tuning period, so the forward test is what settles it.
